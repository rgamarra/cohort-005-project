# Cadence

Cadence is an online course platform. Learners buy courses, work through their lessons, and can ask for their money back shortly after buying.

## Language

### Buying

**Learner**:
A person who buys courses and works through their lessons.
_Avoid_: Student

**Purchase**:
A Learner's payment for a course, which gives them access to it.

### Refunds

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase during which a Learner may raise a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Request**:
A Learner's ask, with their reason, to have the money for a Purchase returned. It is Requested until staff review it, and then becomes Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation, refund (for the ask itself)

**Refund**:
The money returned to a Learner when their Refund Request is Approved. It also ends the Learner's access to the course they bought. A Declined Refund Request produces no Refund.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation

**Chargeback**:
Reserved for a payment dispute the cardholder raises with their card issuer, not with us. Never use it for a Refund Request.
